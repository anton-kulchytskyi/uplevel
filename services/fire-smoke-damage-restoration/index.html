Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration

Comprehensive fire damage restoration from emergency board-up to complete reconstruction, including soot removal and odor elimination.

Fire damage extends far beyond the visible burn marks. Smoke, soot, and water from firefighting efforts can cause additional damage throughout your property. Uplevel Restoration provides complete fire damage restoration services, addressing all aspects of fire damage to return your property to its pre-loss condition. Our IICRC certified technicians understand the complexities of fire restoration and work efficiently to minimize your disruption.

🔥

Soot & Smoke Removal

Specialized cleaning techniques and equipment remove soot from all surfaces without causing further damage.

💨

Odor Elimination

Advanced deodorization methods including ozone treatment and thermal fogging eliminate smoke odors.

🏗️

Structural Repairs

Complete structural rebuilding including framing, roofing, and foundation repairs to restore integrity.

📦

Content Restoration

We clean and restore smoke-damaged belongings, including furniture, clothing, and electronics.

Our Fire Restoration Process

1

Emergency Board-Up

We secure your property immediately to prevent further damage from weather and unauthorized entry.

2

Damage Assessment

Thorough inspection to document all fire, smoke, soot, and water damage for insurance purposes.

3

Soot & Water Removal

We remove soot, smoke residue, and any water damage from firefighting efforts.

4

Restoration & Rebuild

Complete restoration including odor removal, cleaning, repairs, and reconstruction as needed.
