Reconstruction After Loss

Complete property reconstruction services to restore your home or business to pre-loss condition - or better.

After emergency mitigation is complete, the next step is reconstruction. Uplevel Restoration provides full-service reconstruction to return your property to its original condition. As a single-source restoration company, we handle everything from initial damage assessment through final reconstruction, eliminating the need for multiple contractors and ensuring seamless project management.

🏗️

Structural Repairs

Complete structural rebuilding including framing, roofing, and foundation repairs to restore integrity.

🎨

Interior Finishing

Drywall, painting, flooring, and trim work to match or exceed your property's original condition.

⚡

Systems Restoration

Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC repairs or upgrades as needed for complete property restoration.

📑

Project Management

Single-source coordination means one team manages your entire project from start to finish.

Our Reconstruction Process

1
